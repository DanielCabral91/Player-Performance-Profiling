# Synthetic player-profile data

`synthetic_player_profiles.csv` is generated by
`python/generate_synthetic_profiles.py`.

It is fully synthetic and contains no anonymised copies of real youth players.

The V0.2 demo intentionally includes:
- 24 fictitious player IDs;
- 2–3 available evaluation periods per player;
- broad position groups that may change between periods;
- Training / Match / Mixed observation contexts;
- 2–8 observed sessions per player-period;
- 17 ordinal observation attributes;
- some NA ratings representing insufficient observation.

The generator does not encode positional ability stereotypes and does not force a
team-wide positive development trend.
